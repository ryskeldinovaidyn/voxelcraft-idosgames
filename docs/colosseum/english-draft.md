### Brief description — 418 / 500 characters
IDN is a voxel survival sandbox game where players explore, mine resources, craft tools and weapons, build shelters and fight enemies. The prototype includes action-based onboarding, local world saves and dynamic world events. Built on the iDos Games voxel framework, it focuses on the first-session experience. Solana identity, achievements and item ownership are planned; the current gameplay and saves run offchain.

### What are you building, and who is it for? — 971 / 1000 characters
I am building a browser-based voxel survival sandbox for players who enjoy exploration, resource gathering, crafting, building and combat. The prototype includes chunk-based worlds, inventory, tool and weapon recipes, enemies, loot, experience, achievements and local world saves. It builds on the iDos Games host and catalogued voxel module. My product focus is a clearer first session: action-based guidance from movement and gathering wood to crafting a first sword, equipping it and facing an initial enemy. World Pulse adds falling stars, meteor impacts and animal migrations. The initial audience is PC browser players who try indie sandbox and survival games. I plan structured playtests to measure onboarding completion and early-session retention; no external player, revenue or retention figures are claimed. The game runs offchain today. Solana identity, verifiable milestones and selected item ownership are planned, with gameplay first and blockchain second.

### Why did you decide to build this, and why build it now? — 872 / 1000 characters
I enjoy games that let players discover systems and change the world instead of following a fixed campaign. My product hypothesis is that a sandbox needs freedom and a clear first session: gathering wood, opening inventory, crafting a weapon and surviving an initial encounter. I want to test a game-first approach before introducing crypto mechanics. AI-assisted development and the iDos Games voxel framework let me iterate on onboarding, balancing and world events without building the engine from scratch. My experience in sales, operations and customer acquisition since 2016 helps me focus on player needs and practical validation. We are currently checking the prototype and plan external playtests next. Solana is a planned identity and ownership layer, not a prerequisite for the gameplay loop. We have not yet demonstrated onchain achievements or item ownership.

### What technologies are you using or integrating with to build your product?
React 19, TypeScript 5.9, Vite 8, Three.js, iDos Games SDK/host and catalogued voxel module; IndexedDB/localStorage for saves and tutorial knowledge; WebRTC networking via the SDK; Codex for AI-assisted development. Solana web3.js, wallet-adapter-base and Reown Solana adapter are dependencies, but active Solana gameplay integration is not verified. No custom backend or deployed Solana program is present in this source snapshot.
